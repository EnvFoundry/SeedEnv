# Geophysical inversion · 地球物理反演

Formulate forward models and inverse problems.

Specify survey geometry, mesh, parameter mapping, data uncertainty and regularization. Explore how prior structure and sensitivity affect the recovered model rather than treating one inversion as a unique subsurface image.

构造地球物理正演与反演问题。

明确观测几何、网格、参数映射、数据不确定度和正则项。分析先验结构及灵敏度如何影响反演结果。
