# Scientific composition · 科研写作

Write for a reader who needs to understand the work. Read the relevant section and
apply it within the manuscript, without creating a separate planning document.
New SeedEnv manuscripts include this guide as `WRITING.md`.

写给需要理解这项工作的读者。按需阅读下面的内容，直接用于文稿；不另造写作流程文件。
SeedEnv 新建文稿会附带本说明，文件名为 `WRITING.md`。

## Voice and reasoning · 表达与论述

Explain the problem, the idea that advances it, how that idea works, and what follows.
Let each paragraph develop one point and give the next paragraph a reason to exist.
Introduce objects, conditions and notation before relying on them. A short derivation
or concrete example often explains more than a list of abstract qualities. Keep the
steps a reader needs to reconstruct the argument; shorten repetition, not reasoning.

讲清问题、推进问题的思想、它如何起作用，以及由此得到什么。每段发展一个意思，前后段落
彼此承接。先交代对象、条件和记号，再使用它们。用必要的推导或具体例子说明复杂关系。
精简重复，保留读者重建论证所需的步骤。清晰不等于浅显，简洁不等于省略关键推理。

Use concrete nouns and verbs. Replace self-praise about rigor, depth, novelty or
completeness with the construction, explanation, comparison or result that earns it.
Give a supported conclusion directly. State a hypothesis as a hypothesis; name an
unresolved quantity or mechanism when it matters. Do not reflexively downgrade a
result with “merely preliminary” or inflate it into a breakthrough.

用具体的名词和动词。删去关于“顶级、严谨、深入、全面、颠覆性”的自我评价，写出相应的
思想、构造、解释、比较和结果。有依据的结论直接说；假设明确写成假设。确有未解决之处，
说明是哪一个量或机制。不无故弱化已有结果，也不把普通实现夸成突破。

Remove AI filler and defensive rhetoric: ceremonial introductions, stock caveats,
vague hedging, process narration, inflated terminology and repetitive summaries.
Do not decorate ordinary research prose with “diagnosis”, “audit”, “review”, SHA or
hash checks. Use a technical term when it names the actual subject or operation
relevant to the argument. Put necessary operational detail where the reader needs
it; do not invent an audit or checklist to produce a document.

禁止以 AI 套话、空洞黑话或防御性修辞替代内容：仪式化开场、泛泛免责声明、无对象的
模糊限制语、工作过程自述、术语堆叠和重复总结都应删除。“值得注意的是”“需要指出的是”
没有新增含义时直接去掉。“诊断、审计、审查、SHA、哈希校验”不得用来装饰普通研究论述；
确实讨论相应专业对象或技术操作时，使用准确术语。不要为了写作额外生成审计、清单或流程。

A condition that changes a conclusion belongs with that conclusion. Replace “this
may not always work” with the specific regime, measurement range or missing result.
Retain assumptions, uncertainties and contrary observations that change the meaning.
Express them once, precisely, where the reader uses them.

影响结论的条件应与结论一起说清。“并不总是有效”应落实为具体工作区间、测量范围或缺失
的结果。会改变含义的假设、不确定性和相反观测要保留，在相关位置准确说明一次即可。

Vary sentence length with its purpose. A short sentence can carry the conclusion;
a longer one can connect the mechanism and its consequences. Keep important verbs
close to their subjects and use parallel forms for parallel ideas. Headings mark
real changes of question; tables compare aligned facts. Neither should break a
connected explanation into fragments.

句长服从内容：短句落定判断，较长的句子展开联系。避免整段同一节奏、连续碎句和层层
嵌套的长句。标题标明问题的转换，表格用于可比较的信息，连贯的解释保留为自然段。

## Papers and research reports · 论文与研究报告

Organize the work around its question and actual contribution: an explanation,
theorem, construction, measurement, method, replication, synthesis or negative result.
Compare with the closest relevant work under comparable conditions. Explain why a
derivation or experiment is needed before giving its outcome. A theoretical report
should expose the decisive construction; an experimental report should connect the
measurement to the proposed mechanism.

围绕研究问题和实际贡献组织内容。贡献可以是解释、定理、构造、测量、方法、复现、综合
或否定性结果。比较应对应相近工作和可比条件。先说明一项推导或实验解决什么问题，再
给出结果；理论报告突出关键构造，实验报告把测量与所讨论的机制连接起来。

The report template offers background and problem, central idea, theory and methods,
experiments or computation, results and discussion, conclusions and applications.
Adapt or combine these roles. A proof does not need an invented experiment; a
replication does not need a fabricated novelty claim. Keep central mathematical
and physical reasoning in the main text. Appendices hold supporting detail.

报告模板提供背景与问题、核心思想、理论与方法、实验或计算、结果与讨论、结论与应用等
内容位置，可以合并或调整。证明工作无需硬配实验，复现工作无需伪造创新。中心数学推导、
物理解释和结果分析留在正文，辅助细节放入附录。

Build the abstract around the question, what was done, the result and its meaning;
choose a title that identifies the subject and finding. Introduce figures where they
advance the argument. Explain quantities, axes, units and comparisons, then the
result's meaning. Avoid figure-by-figure narration that merely reads labels aloud.
Distinguish an observed association from an established cause.

摘要交代问题、工作、结果与意义，题名指出研究对象与发现。图表在推进论证的位置出现，
交代物理量、坐标、单位和比较关系，再解释结果的意义。不要把图注逐字复述成正文。
观察到的关联与已经建立的因果关系分别表达。

For a decision report, state the decision and the comparison that determines it.
Explain which conditions change the choice. Give a concrete recommendation when
the information supports one, with the reason and the setting in which it applies.

面向决策的报告要说明所做选择及其依据，指出哪些条件会改变选择。已有信息足够时，直接
给出建议、原因和适用情形。

## Books and sustained explanations · 书籍与长篇讲解

Choose the reader's starting point and the understanding each chapter should add.
Order material by conceptual dependence or a purposeful narrative. Introduce a
question through a case the reader can reason about, develop the governing relation,
and return to the case with a prediction or a new explanation. Show where an analogy
maps to the model and where it stops. Keep necessary mathematics in view.

先确定读者已经知道什么，以及本章要让读者理解什么。按概念依赖或清楚的叙述线索安排
章节。从可以思考的具体问题进入，建立支配关系，再回到原问题得到预测或解释。类比要
交代与模型的对应关系及其终点；必要的数学不能被比喻替代。

Use a worked example, then vary one consequential condition to reveal what generalizes.
Keep useful proofs and counterexamples even when they take space. Maintain terminology
across chapters and retrieve earlier definitions when needed. Transitions should expose
a connection or an unresolved question. End with a result or a sharper question,
without repeating the whole chapter.

完整算清一个例子，再改变一个关键条件，说明哪些关系仍然成立。保留有用的证明与反例。
跨章节统一术语，需要时回到先前定义。过渡应说明联系或尚未解答的问题；章节结尾可以
落在一个结果或更准确的问题上，不必再复述全章。

## Chinese, English and revision · 中英文与修改

Chinese prose can connect ideas through shared context, concise phrasing and rhythm.
Preserve explicit causes and referents wherever omission would make reasoning ambiguous.
English prose often benefits from explicit subjects and verbs, known information before
new information, and parallel syntax for comparisons. Use the active voice when it
clarifies who acts; use the passive when the operation or object is the topic. These
are choices for readers, not rules that flatten either language into one voice.

中文可借意合、凝练和节奏形成气韵，给读者留出理解空间；省略会造成歧义时，因果关系和
指代必须说全。英文通常需要更明确的主谓关系、由已知到新信息的推进和整齐的平行结构。
主动语态便于说明谁做了什么，研究对象或操作本身是焦点时也可使用被动语态。这些是为
读者作出的选择，不是把中英文写成同一种腔调。

Revise substance and reasoning before polishing sentences. Fix a local problem
locally when the surrounding argument works. Translate relationships and register,
not word order; keep quantities, units, terminology and claim strength consistent.
Read a difficult paragraph aloud to hear overloaded clauses and monotonous rhythm.

先修改内容和推理，再润色句子；局部问题局部解决。翻译传递关系和语气，不搬运词序；
数量、单位、术语和结论强度保持一致。难读的段落可以朗读，听出气息过长的分句和单调节奏。

## Worked examples · 写法示例

These are original teaching examples. Report numbers are invented to demonstrate
composition and must not be used as research findings.

以下段落是本项目原创的写法示例。报告中的数字是假设值，只用于说明表达方式，不得作为
真实研究结果使用。

### Replace decoration with information · 用信息替代修辞

| 空泛写法 / Empty wording | 具体写法 / Concrete wording |
| --- | --- |
| 我们对模型进行了全面诊断与严格审计，形成可靠的研究闭环。 | 拟合残差随温度升高而增大；加入温度项后，这一趋势消失。 |
| 值得注意的是，该方法在一定程度上可能具有优势。 | 在这 20 个相同测试实例中，B 的平均误差比 A 低 25%。 |
| 本文进行了 SHA 校验，确保结论可信。 | 数值解与解析解的差随步长减半缩小至约四分之一，与二阶误差一致。 |
| It is important to note that the approach may potentially be beneficial. | Across the same 20 test cases, B reduced mean error by 25% relative to A. |
| A comprehensive diagnostic review was conducted to ensure robustness. | Residuals grew with temperature; adding a temperature term removed the trend. |
| The system leverages a sophisticated framework to facilitate analysis. | The solver computes the field; integrating its intensity gives the transmitted power. |

Each replacement assumes the stated observation actually exists. Otherwise remove
the unsupported sentence or state the concrete question that remains.

这些改写以确有相应观测为前提。没有这项结果时，删掉无依据的句子，或说明仍待回答的
具体问题；不要为了消除空话而编造数据。

### Book passage: mechanism to equation · 书籍：从机制到方程

A capacitor discharges through a resistor. As its voltage falls, the current falls
too, so the same time interval removes progressively less charge. With constant
resistance R and capacitance C, the voltage obeys C dV/dt = −V/R. Starting from V₀,
it follows that V(t) = V₀ exp(−t/RC). The time RC has a concrete meaning: after this
interval, the voltage is 1/e of its starting value. Doubling either R or C doubles
the decay time.

电容通过电阻放电。电压下降，电流也随之减小，因此相同时间内流失的电荷越来越少。
电阻 R 和电容 C 不变时，电压满足 C dV/dt = −V/R。由初始电压 V₀ 得到
V(t) = V₀ exp(−t/RC)。时间 RC 因而有了具体含义：经过这段时间，电压降到初值的 1/e。
电阻或电容增大一倍，衰减所需的时间也增大一倍。

This connects mechanism, equation, solution and consequence. Use the progression
when it helps; it is not a required sentence pattern.

这一段依次连接机制、方程、解和可观察的后果。可借鉴这种推进方式，无需照搬句式。

### Report passage: result to judgment · 报告：从结果到判断

In the same 20 test cases, method B reduced mean normalized error from 0.24 to 0.18,
while mean runtime rose from 30 to 42 seconds per case. The error reduction is 25%;
the runtime increase is 40%. For offline analysis where either runtime is acceptable,
B gives the lower error. If mean runtime must stay at or below 30 seconds, A meets
that requirement in this comparison and B does not. These averages do not establish
a per-case latency guarantee; that question requires the individual runtimes.

在相同的 20 个测试实例中，方法 B 将平均归一化误差从 0.24 降至 0.18，平均单次耗时从
30 秒增至 42 秒。误差降低 25%，耗时增加 40%。离线分析若能接受两者的耗时，B 的误差
更低；若要求平均耗时不超过 30 秒，本次比较中 A 满足要求，B 不满足。平均值不能决定
每次运行是否都符合时限，判断后者需要各次运行的耗时。
