# Many-particle dynamics · 多粒子动力学

Simulate particles with explicit interactions and integrators.

Choose interaction cutoff, boundary conditions and units together. Separate equilibration from sampling and record thermodynamic observables at a cadence suited to their correlation time.

具有明确相互作用与积分器的多粒子仿真。

共同确定相互作用截断、边界和单位。平衡阶段与采样阶段分开，按观测量相关时间选择记录间隔。

Software / 软件：HOOMD-blue (`environment-hoomd`).

Use `seedenv context many-particle-dynamics` for software entries and setup on this host.
